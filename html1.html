05 Beyond the Standard Model — what it can't explain

Dark matter

~27% of the universe's mass-energy doesn't match any particle here. Gravitationally real, otherwise invisible.

Dark energy

~68% of the universe is accelerating cosmic expansion — no Standard Model particle or field explains it.

Neutrino mass

The Standard Model originally predicted massless neutrinos. Oscillation experiments proved they have tiny mass — the theory had to be patched.

Matter–antimatter asymmetry

The Big Bang should have made equal matter and antimatter, annihilating into pure energy. Something tipped the balance — we don't fully know what.

No quantum gravity

Gravity has no place in this table. There's no known graviton, and no consistent quantum theory of gravity yet.

Why these masses?

The Higgs mechanism explains how particles get mass, not why each one's coupling — and therefore its mass — has the exact value it does.
